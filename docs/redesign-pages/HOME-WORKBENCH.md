## Approved behavior

- Calendar-first, edge-connected desktop surface with a 366px attention dock.
- Action Items, Milestones Due and Revenue Alerts remain distinct task regions.
- Calendar navigation, day refresh, customer matching and quick-create keep their
  original associations and local call dates.
- Shared note/milestone/action-item previews retain protected drafts, nested
  editors, keyboard access and parent context.
- Freshness/status indicates actual reads, failures and confirmed refresh state;
  it does not turn failed lookups into fresh data.
- Home uses its existing connected grid, not another page's generic split layout.
- Native buckets/review/revenue previews use shared controllers and styles.
- App branding stays in the shell; Help/About owns legal/app information.
