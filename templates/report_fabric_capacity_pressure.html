How pressure is identified

Investigation heuristics, not official alerts: throttling recorded >0 seconds; latest-hour utilization ≥80% (near limit) or ≥100% (overload); peak 30-second utilization within that hour ≥100%. Missing metrics remain unknown. Utilization percentages are never summed.

The 30-second peak covers the latest available hour, not the full 15-day period. It can be lower than the 15-day average.
