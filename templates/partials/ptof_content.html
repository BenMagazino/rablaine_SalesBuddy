How to read P to F

P-SKU is Power BI Premium capacity; F-SKU is Microsoft Fabric capacity. SA Expiration $ is the source's expiring dollar baseline. A blank amount is not a confirmed zero.

The published calculation legend applies these rules in order:

  1. Both P-SKU and F-SKU renewal percentages are at least 75%: PSKU Renewed + New Fabric.
  2. Only P-SKU is at least 75%: PSKU Renewed.
  3. Only F-SKU is at least 75%: P-to-F Renewed.
  4. F-SKU is at least 30% but below 75%: Partial P-to-F.
  5. Otherwise: Pending Renewal.

The model also returns Not Renewed, which its legend does not explain. Sales Buddy keeps the actual source status rather than guessing that rule. If an account has multiple distinct source renewal statuses, all are listed together without duplicating its subscription rows.
