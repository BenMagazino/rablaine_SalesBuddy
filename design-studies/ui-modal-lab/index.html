Round twelve / Centered modals
Open Graphite milestone modal

A focused window, not another sidebar.

Centered modal treatments for the selected themes. Action items use a compact dialog with completion and edit controls. Milestones and notes use wider, scrollable detail layouts with persistent headers and footers. All content is fictional; the live homepage was inspected for structure only.

Compare the workspace or its menus. Click a sample for the interactive preview.
Dark mode uses restrained customer fills and bright status rails. Light mode translates those hues into tinted fills, dark text, and deeper rails; ghost meetings keep dashed outlines and striped rails in both.
All pairs preserve the layout, typography, customer assignments and highlighting, status precedence, and 23 fictional entries. The preview toolbar switches mode within the selected family.
Click an action item, milestone, saved calendar note, or New note to open a centered modal. Escape, the close button, or the backdrop dismiss it; keyboard focus stays inside and returns to the trigger.
Edit, completion, and save controls are local demonstrations only. Full page and MSX buttons never navigate outside the preview.
